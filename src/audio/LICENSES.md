# Street radio audio provenance

The currently shipped score and effects are original Game-Lab compositions and synthesized sound designs, authored by `tools/build_street_audio.py`. They contain no external samples, no recordings from Ice Cream.mp4, and no borrowed melodies or branded console sounds. `provenance.json` records the encoded files and WAV master hashes. Source masters are in `assets/audio-street/masters`.

The original v0.64 CC0 files, exact hashes, source links and credits remain intact in `assets/audio-legacy/LICENSES.md` and `assets/audio-legacy/provenance.json`. Those historical files are not embedded in the new game build. Their original CC0 license is not relabelled as authorship of the new compositions.

New material is original project material; this record does not apply a new third-party license or change the repository's licensing terms.
