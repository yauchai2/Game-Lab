This palette mixes CC0 recordings and project-original compositions/design. No audio was taken from Ice Cream.mp4,Into Moon's Dream Logic or Lionclad's Dorephin Shopping Center. Those works are references, not licensed soundtrack assets.

## Project-original material

All six current music recordings are CC0; the earlier synthetic `underpass`/`rush` arrangements are replaced. Thirteen common hit/movement/UI cues now use Kenney recordings without the previous synthesized layers. Original synthesized layers in the remaining effects remain credited to Game-Lab; per-asset records distinguish original and mixed-license files. The prior palette remains recoverable from immutable source commit `96ec21e3587bd1c7892b01bfc12462b834475380`, identified in `assets/audio-online/pre-release-manifest.json`.

The older17 CC0 source files and their existing provenance/licenses in `assets/audio-legacy` remain unmodified and are not reinserted into the runtime palette. This record does not relicense the repository or claim external recordings as project-original.
